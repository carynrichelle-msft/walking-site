Chapter One · My Story

The thread that runs through everything I do.

I've spent my career in tech, navigating growth, acquisitions, and constant change.

What I learned along the way: when the environment is chaotic, the people who thrive are the ones who build their own structure. So I did. I built systems for how I work, how I manage complexity, and how I show up for customers.

At Microsoft, I started teaching others to do the same, turning those workflows into repeatable frameworks that reduce friction and accelerate outcomes.

I don't just adapt to complexity. I build systems that make it manageable, for myself, my team, and the customers I serve.

That's the thread. Three pillars hold it together: build systems that work, bring people with you, and scale what works.
